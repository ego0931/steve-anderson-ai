Personal vs. Commercial

Personal is for gifts, family, birthdays, memorials and celebrations. Commercial is for businesses, ads, promotions, or anything that makes money. Personal is half the price of commercial.

Full Rights vs. Creator Keeps

Full Rights: the finished work is yours to use, share and build on (you may add to it, but not alter the original). Creator Keeps: you get to use and share it, and I keep the rights, so I may show it as a sample of my work. Full details are in the written agreement.
