Personal vs. Commercial

Personal is for gifts, family, birthdays, memorials and celebrations. Commercial is for businesses, ads, promotions, or anything that makes money. Personal is about half the price of commercial.

Full Rights vs. Creator Keeps

Full Rights: the finished work is yours to use, share and build on (you may add to it, but not alter the original). Creator Keeps: you get to use and share it, and I keep the rights, so I may show it as a sample of my work. Full details are in the written agreement.

FAQ

What does "AI-assisted" mean?

I use AI services and AI equipment as creative tools. I personally guide every project: shaping the idea with you, writing and directing the song and visuals, and checking everything before you see it.

How long does it take?

Your sample arrives within 7 days after I have everything needed to finish: every item on your checklist, your signed forms, and your deposit. The 7 days start then, not at payment. You have 5 days to approve or send fixes, and fixes come within 2 more days. Final files come right after you approve and pay.

What do I send you?

Your story, names (with spelling and pronunciation), the mood or style you like, and any photos. Each package has its own list on the What I Need From You checklist (PDF). Don't attach photos to the form; I'll reply with an easy way to send photos and files.

Can I use it at a funeral, wedding, or business?

Yes. Personal covers family events like funerals, weddings and birthdays. Anything for a business, ad, or promotion needs a Commercial package.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, for personal jobs: 4 payments every 2 weeks, 3 monthly payments, or 25% down then weekly. Final files are delivered once paid in full.† Not available for commercial jobs.

Who owns the finished work?

Full Rights: I transfer the rights I hold to you. Creator Keeps: you can use and share it, and I keep ownership. The full details are in the written agreement you get before paying.
